Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your Umrah package?

Umrah visa, return air ticket, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, complete transport and ziaraat in both holy cities. Food is not included unless stated in your package.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve pilgrims from Warburton, Nankana Sahib, Shahkot, Sangla Hill, Sheikhupura, Lahore, Faisalabad and surrounding areas of Punjab.

Can you make a package for my family / my dates?

Yes — group, family and custom packages are all available. Tell us your dates and number of travellers on WhatsApp and we will build your package.

How do I confirm my booking?

Choose your package on WhatsApp, submit your passport/documents as guided, and your visa, ticket and hotels are confirmed for you in writing.
